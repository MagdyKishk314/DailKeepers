FAQ

Questions owners ask us

Do I need a custom CRM?

Usually not at first. Most small teams start with the automated Google Sheet and move to a custom CRM when they outgrow it. On the call we tell you which one fits.

Can we start with the sheet and upgrade later?

Yes. The sheet is designed as the way in, and the custom CRM is built from the same lead flow, so nothing is thrown away.

Does it connect to my dialer?

The sheet loads your dialer exports. The custom CRM can connect through your dialer's webhooks or API where it offers them. We check yours on the call.

Who owns the data?

You do. The sheet lives in your Google account and the CRM holds your data, not ours.

What does monthly support cover?

Fixes when your dialer or Google changes something, so the tool keeps working. New features or extra work are quoted separately.

How is it priced?

A one-time setup fee plus monthly support. We quote after a 30-minute call, once we know how your leads move through your team.
