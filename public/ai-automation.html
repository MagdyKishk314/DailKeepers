The problem

Nobody has time to listen to every call.

So wrong dispositions slip through, coaching is guesswork, and problems show up days later. These automations do the checking for you.

Disposition check

What it does
Compares each call with the disposition your agent picked, and flags the ones that do not match.
What you get
A list of mismatches to fix, so good leads are not closed early or recycled the wrong way.

Call scoring

What it does
Scores a weekly sample of calls on your own checklist.
What you get
A short coaching note per caller, so you know who needs help and with what.

Live alerts

What it does
Watches wait time, drop rate and flagged numbers while you dial.
What you get
A message in Telegram or Slack when something drifts, plus a weekly report built automatically.

FAQ

Questions owners ask us

Do you need our call recordings?

Only for the call checks and scoring, and only with your written consent. Before we start we agree in writing where recordings are stored and who can see them.

Who pays for the AI usage?

You do, directly. The automations run on your own AI account and keys, so usage is billed to you at cost and the tool and data stay yours.

Does the AI make mistakes?

Yes, sometimes. That is why every automation starts in alert-only mode: it flags and scores, and a person on your team decides what changes. Scores are coaching input, not a verdict.

Which dialers does it work with?

Dialers export recordings and dispositions in different ways, so we check how yours works on the call before we promise anything.

How is it priced?

A one-time setup fee plus monthly support. AI usage is billed to your own account. We quote after a short call, based on what you want automated.
