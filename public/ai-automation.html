Examples

A few things we can build.

Examples to show the idea. Every automation is built around your own process, so yours may look different.

AI Leads Auto Qualifier

What it does
Reads every new lead from your forms, ads or lists, checks it against your criteria and scores it.
What you get
Qualified leads go straight to the dialer; the rest are flagged or held, so agents stop calling bad data.

AI Agent Coach

What it does
Listens to random calls throughout the day and checks them against your script and quality checklist.
What you get
A warning the same day when an agent goes off track, and a training plan for each agent, without listening to every call yourself.

AI Daily Report

What it does
Pulls the day's numbers from your dialer and writes a plain-English summary of what changed.
What you get
A short message in Telegram or Slack every morning, with what needs your attention first.

FAQ

Questions owners ask us

What can you automate?

Most repetitive, rule-based work around your dialer: qualifying and routing leads, reviewing calls and dispositions, writing reports, follow-up reminders and alerts. On the free call we look at where your team spends time by hand and tell you what is worth automating first.

Do we need technical staff?

No. We build it, connect it to the tools you already use and show your team how to run it. Monthly support covers fixes and adjustments.

Who pays for the AI usage?

You do, directly. Automations run on your own AI account and keys, so usage is billed to you at cost and the automations and data stay yours.

Does the AI make mistakes?

Sometimes. That is why automations start by suggesting and flagging, and a person on your team approves before anything changes. We only let an automation act on its own once you have seen it work.

What about call recordings and privacy?

Recordings are only processed with your written consent, and we agree in writing where they are stored and who can see them before we start.

How is it priced?

A one-time setup fee per automation plus monthly support. AI usage is billed to your own account. We quote after a short call, once we know what you want automated.
