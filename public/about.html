About Dialkeepers

We keep your dialers dialing.

Dialkeepers is a dialer management company for small call centers. Our Keepers run the technical side of your dialer on a written SLA, so you stop buying more numbers and data to fix problems that come from the settings.

Who we work with

Built for small call centers.

We work with owners of small outbound call centers who don't have a dedicated dialer admin. Most come to us after trying the obvious fix: buying more numbers and more data. Usually the problem is in how the dialer is run, and that is the part we take off your hands.

How we work

Written down, so you can check it.

  • Keepers

    Dialer admins in Egypt

    Full-time Keepers who know the dialers and look after yours day to day.

  • SOPs

    Written procedures

    Every task follows a written procedure, so the work is consistent and easy to check.

  • SLA

    Commitments in writing

    Response times and reports are written into your agreement before work starts.

  • Read-only

We don't guarantee sales or contact rates. Those also depend on your offer, your leads and your agents. We commit to the work we control.
