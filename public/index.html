Three things that quietly drain a dialer budget.

Most owners come to us after trying the obvious fix: buying more. Here is what is usually behind it.

  • Are you buying more phone numbers than you should?

    Usually: Numbers get flagged as spam or stop being answered, so the quick fix is to buy more. But calls are often spread badly: some numbers burn out while others sit idle, and flagged ones are never retired.

    What we do: We match your number count to your agents and call volume, rest or retire flagged numbers, rotate the rest evenly, and cancel the extras, so you only pay for numbers you use.

  • Are you burning data with no leads to show for it?

    Usually: The same leads get dialed again and again, dead and duplicate numbers stay in the list, and nothing limits the attempts. Fresh data runs out fast, so you buy more, and it burns the same way.

    What we do: We clean and dedupe lists before they load, set attempt limits and calling times, and recycle leads on a schedule, so each lead gets a fair number of tries and no more.

  • Are your wait times long, or different every day?

    Usually: Dialing speed is set once and never adjusted. Too slow and agents sit idle. Too fast and calls drop. An empty lead queue or a weak number makes it swing from hour to hour.

    What we do: We tune dialing speed to your agents and answer rates, keep the lead queue fed, and keep watching it, so the wait is the same on a Tuesday as on a Friday.

Questions owners ask us

What does Dialkeepers do?

We manage your dialer for you. We right-size the phone numbers (DIDs) you pay for, protect your data from being burned, keep dialing speed steady so agents aren't waiting, answer your requests within 15 minutes, and send a weekly performance report. You keep your dialer. We run the settings.

What is a DID?

A DID is a phone number your dialer calls from. Prospects see it as your caller ID. Too many means you pay for numbers that sit idle. Too few, or poorly rotated, means the ones you have get flagged as spam and stop being answered.

How many DIDs do I actually need?

It depends on your agents, how much you dial and how often numbers get flagged. On the audit call we look at what you own, what is in use and what is getting flagged, and tell you the count that fits. Buying more numbers rarely fixes a number problem that comes from how they are used.

Why do I keep running out of data?

Usually the same leads are dialed too many times, dead and duplicate numbers are still in the list, and nothing limits the attempts. Buying more data without fixing that burns the new data the same way. We clean lists, set attempt limits and recycle leads on a schedule.

Why are my agents waiting so long, or why does it change every day?

Dialing speed is usually set once and left alone. If it is too slow, agents sit idle. If it is too fast, calls drop. Lead queues running low and weak numbers make it swing during the day. We tune the speed to your agents and answer rates and keep it steady.

Is the audit really free?
