What we fix

Three things that quietly drain a dialer budget.

Most owners come to us after trying the obvious fix: buying more. Here is what is usually behind it.

Wasting money on DIDs?

Usually
Numbers get flagged as spam or stop being answered, so the quick fix is to buy more. But calls are often spread badly: some numbers burn out while others sit idle, and flagged ones are never retired.
What we do
We match your number count to your agents and call volume, rest or retire flagged numbers, rotate the rest evenly, and cancel the extras, so you only pay for numbers you use.

Burning through your data?

Usually
The same leads get dialed again and again, dead and duplicate numbers stay in the list, and nothing limits the attempts. Fresh data runs out fast, so you buy more, and it burns the same way.
What we do
We clean and dedupe lists before they load, set attempt limits and calling times, and recycle leads on a schedule, so each lead gets a fair number of tries and no more.

Agents left waiting?

Usually
Dialing speed is set once and never adjusted. Too slow and agents sit idle. Too fast and calls drop. An empty lead queue or a weak number makes it swing from hour to hour.
What we do
We tune dialing speed to your agents and answer rates, keep the lead queue fed, and keep watching it, so the wait is the same on a Tuesday as on a Friday.

FAQ

Questions owners ask us

What does Dialkeepers do?

We manage your dialer for you. We right-size the phone numbers (DIDs) you pay for, protect your data from being burned, keep dialing speed steady so agents aren't waiting, answer your requests within 15 minutes, and send a weekly performance report. You keep your dialer. We run the settings.

What is a DID?

A DID is a phone number your dialer calls from. Prospects see it as your caller ID. Too many means you pay for numbers that sit idle. Too few, or poorly rotated, means the ones you have get flagged as spam and stop being answered.

How many DIDs do I actually need?

It depends on your agents, how much you dial and how often numbers get flagged. On the audit call we look at what you own, what is in use and what is getting flagged, and tell you the count that fits. Buying more numbers rarely fixes a number problem that comes from how they are used.

Why do I keep running out of data?

Usually the same leads are dialed too many times, dead and duplicate numbers are still in the list, and nothing limits the attempts. Buying more data without fixing that burns the new data the same way. We clean lists, set attempt limits and recycle leads on a schedule.

Why are my agents waiting so long, or why does it change every day?

Dialing speed is usually set once and left alone. If it is too slow, agents sit idle. If it is too fast, calls drop. Lead queues running low and weak numbers make it swing during the day. We tune the speed to your agents and answer rates and keep it steady.

Is the audit really free?

Yes. We get on a call, ask about your data, numbers, seats and the problems you've had, and explain how we would fix them. There is no cost and no commitment.

Which dialers do you work with?

ReadyMode, Convoso, GoHighLevel, CallTools, VICIdial, Enzo Dialer and any other power or predictive dialer. You keep the dialer you have. We work inside it.

What access do you need?

None for the first call. If we work together, we start with read-only access to review your setup, then agree in writing exactly what Keepers may change.

Where are Keepers based?

Keepers are dialer admins based in Egypt, working from written procedures.

How is it priced?

We quote after the call, based on your dialer, your number of seats and what needs managing.

Do you guarantee sales or contact rates?

No. We commit to what we control: replies within 15 minutes, a weekly performance report, and managing your numbers and data so you don't overbuy. Sales still depend on your offer, your lead quality and your agents.
